Data Visualizations

A sample of data visualizations from an investigative story about physical education compliance in Chicago Public Schools.

The story is currently being pitched to local outlets. Information and graphics are not to be used for distribution. Further details about the story are available upon request.

GeoJSON & Flourish

I obtained each of the 473 elementary schools' average days of P.E. from the Illinois Report Card, sorted schools by neigbhorhood and found the median average days of P.E. for each neighborhood. I then visualized this data using a map created with GeoJSON in Flourish.

Canva

Looking at the Bronzeville neighborhood specifically, I created a histogram in Canva to illustrate the frequency of noncompliance, even within a specific neighborhood.
