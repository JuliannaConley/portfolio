About Julianna

Originally from Orange County, CA, Julianna Conley is a MSJ candidate at Northwestern's Medill School of Journalism, specializing in magazine writing and concentrating in data journalism. She graduated cum laude from the University of Notre Dame's honors program with majors in Sociology and Pre-Health Studies and a minor in Journalism, Ethics, and Democracy. After completing her undergraduate studies, she earned her Master's of Education while teaching through AmeriCorps.

Julianna’s work focuses on education, community and human-centered features. She’s currently working with Jill Barshay from The Hechinger Report as an education reporting fellow in the Medill Education Journalism Collaborative. Most recently, her work has been published in Block Club Chicago.

A versatile writer, she previously wrote a consistently trending column for The Observer, creative nonfiction for an academic journal, and PR articles for the University of Notre Dame’s Office of Development. In addition to written media, Julianna’s developed multiple podcasts, two of which won "Best Podcast" and "3rd Best Podcast" from the Indiana Collegiate Press Awards.
