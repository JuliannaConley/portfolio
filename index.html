About Julianna

Julianna Conley is a journalist from Southern California, now living and working in Washington, D.C., as an intern for NPR’s Editorial Graphics and Education Desk teams.

Julianna recently graduated from Northwestern University with her Master of Journalism, specializing in magazine writing and data journalism. Before attending Northwestern, she graduated cum laude from the University of Notre Dame's honors program with majors in Sociology and Pre-Health Studies and a minor in Journalism, Ethics, and Democracy. After completing her undergraduate studies, she earned her Master of Education while teaching elementary school through AmeriCorps.

Described by the Association for Education in Journalism and Mass Communication (AEJMC) as “funny, warm and ironic, but also edifying,” Julianna’s work focuses on education, data visualization and human-centered features. She’s previously worked with Jill Barshay from The Hechinger Report as an education reporting fellow in the Medill Education Journalism Collaborative. Most recently, her work has been published in Block Club Chicago, winning second place in the AEJMC Awards’ service journalism category.

In addition to written media, Julianna’s developed multiple podcasts, two of which won "Best Podcast" and "3rd Best Podcast" from the Indiana Collegiate Press Awards.
